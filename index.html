Research

Optimization for classroom allocation

GOAL – Group of Optimization and Algorithms, UFOP · 2025–2026 · undergraduate thesis

  • Structured real institutional data on classes and rooms and wrote the mathematical model of the problem.
  • Built constructive heuristics, local search and simulated annealing in C++, with a cost function that weighs room capacity, preferences and scheduling constraints.

Virtual reality and brain–computer interfaces for the mining industry

XR4GOOD Lab, UFOP · 2024–2025 · PIBITI research grant

  • Built a VR simulator of ore-wagon loading in Unity/C#, controlled through an 8-channel EEG headset (code-modulated visual evoked potentials) on a Meta Quest Pro.
  • Ran the study with 26 participants and analysed it with SUS, TAM and ANOVA: usability scored 77 (the acceptable threshold is 68), and prior VR experience changed how people rated the system.

Towards a VR-BCI Based System to Evaluate the Effectiveness of Immersive Technologies in Industry. ICEIS 2025. doi:10.5220/0013483800003929

Would You Mind? Evaluating User Acceptance and Usability of a Brain-Interface System in an Industrial Scenario. SVR 2025. doi:10.1109/SVR67689.2025.00046
